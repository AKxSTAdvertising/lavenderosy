📌 How Vitamin C Serums Work for Glowing Skin

Vitamin C is a powerful antioxidant that helps:
✔ Reduce pigment and dark spots
✔ Protect skin from daily environmental stressors
✔ Boost collagen production for firmer skin
✔ Improve texture and even out skin tone

Expert dermatologists recommend serums with 10–20% Vitamin C concentration for effective results without irritation, especially when paired with sunscreen daily.

🧴 Tips for Choosing the Right Vitamin C Serum in India

  1. Check the concentration: Serums with 10–15% Vitamin C are generally effective yet gentle for most skin types.
  2. Stability matters: Look for derivatives like ethyl ascorbic acid or stabilized Vitamin C to prevent oxidation.
  3. Suitable texture for your skin: Lightweight, gel-like serums absorb better in humid climates like India’s.
  4. Combine with sunscreen: Always use sunscreen in the morning to protect your Vitamin C-treated skin from UV damage.

🧡 How to Use Vitamin C Serum for Best Results

✔ Apply after cleansing and toning
✔ Use 3–5 drops on clean skin
✔ Follow with a moisturizer
✔ Finish with broad-spectrum sunscreen during daytime

Consistency is key — visible brightness and improved texture typically appear within 4–8 weeks with regular use.
