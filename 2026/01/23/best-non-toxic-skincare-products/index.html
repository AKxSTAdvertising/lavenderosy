📌 FAQs (SEO Boost & Reader Help)

Q: What does non-toxic skincare mean?
A: Non-toxic skincare uses clean ingredients, avoids harsh chemicals (free from parabens, sulfates, silicones, synthetic fragrance), and focuses on gentle, skin-safe formulation.

Q: Is ‘natural’ always non-toxic?
A: Not always — some natural ingredients can still irritate. Always check labels and patch test.

Q: Can non-toxic products work for acne?
A: Yes! Many clean products use botanical actives like tea tree, niacinamide, and witch hazel which help with breakouts naturally.
