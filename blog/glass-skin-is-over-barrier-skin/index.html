What the skin barrier does

The outermost layer of skin helps retain moisture and protect against irritants. When it is stressed, skin may feel tight, flaky or sensitive. Supporting that protective function is often more useful than chasing an unrealistic glossy finish.

Keep the routine simple

A mild cleanser, moisturizer and broad-spectrum sunscreen provide a practical foundation. Introduce stronger active ingredients gradually and notice how your skin responds.

Comfort is the new glow

Real skin has pores and texture. A healthy-looking complexion does not need to be flawless or reflective. Persistent irritation should be discussed with a dermatologist.
