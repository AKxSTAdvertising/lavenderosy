Winter Skincare: Finding a Moisturizer That Works

Simple ways to support dry skin when the weather changes.

A thoughtful place to begin

Simple ways to support dry skin when the weather changes. The most meaningful changes often start with a little curiosity and a willingness to try something different.

Why winter skin needs extra care

Cold air, indoor heating and long hot showers can make skin feel dry and tight. During winter, the goal is to reduce moisture loss and support the skin barrier. Notice which areas need extra care and avoid changing every product at once.

Ingredients worth understanding

Humectants such as glycerin attract water, while emollients soften the skin and occlusives help reduce moisture loss. Ceramides, squalane and petrolatum are common ingredients found in moisturizers. Texture and personal tolerance matter as much as any ingredient list.

Choose a texture that suits you

Dry skin may prefer a richer cream, while combination or oily skin may feel more comfortable with a lighter lotion. Fragrance-free formulas can be a sensible starting point for easily irritated skin. Patch-test new products before using them widely.

A simple winter routine

Cleanse gently with lukewarm water, moisturize while skin is slightly damp and use daytime sun protection. Avoid over-exfoliating and seek professional advice if dryness becomes painful, cracked or persistently inflamed.
